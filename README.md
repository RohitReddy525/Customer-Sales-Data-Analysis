Customer & Sales Data Analysis System

📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes customer and sales data to identify revenue trends, customer behavior, product performance, regional performance, and payment preferences.

The project combines Excel Dashboard Analysis and Python Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) to generate meaningful business insights from 5,000 e-commerce transactions.

🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance
- Identify top-performing product categories
- Compare regional revenue performance
- Analyze customer value and purchasing behavior
- Segment customers based on revenue
- Analyze payment method preferences
- Identify monthly revenue trends
- Understand relationships between sales variables

💡 Key Business Insights

- Total revenue generated was approximately ₹51.10 lakh from 5,000 orders.
- The average order value was approximately ₹1,021.96.
- Electronics generated the highest revenue among product categories.
- The West region generated the highest regional revenue.
- Card was the highest-revenue payment method.
- High-value customers contributed a significant portion of overall customer revenue.
- Unit price and quantity showed positive relationships with revenue.
- Discount showed a weak negative relationship with revenue.
- Monthly analysis was used to understand sales trends over time.

👥 Customer Segmentation

Customers were classified into three segments based on total revenue:

Segment| Revenue Range
Low Value| ₹0 – ₹3,000
Medium Value| ₹3,001 – ₹7,000
High Value| Above ₹7,000

This segmentation can help businesses identify valuable customers and design targeted marketing strategies.
